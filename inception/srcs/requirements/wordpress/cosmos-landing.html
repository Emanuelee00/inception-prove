Constellations
Ancient star patterns that guide us through the night sky

Orion

The Hunter — one of the most recognizable patterns in the night sky. Visible from both hemispheres during winter months.

Ursa Major

The Great Bear — contains the famous Big Dipper asterism, whose two outer stars point directly toward Polaris, the North Star.

Cassiopeia

The Queen — a distinctive W-shape that circles the North Celestial Pole and is visible year-round from northern latitudes.

Scorpius

The Scorpion — a brilliant summer constellation anchored by the red supergiant Antares, meaning "rival of Mars."

Leo

The Lion — a spring constellation featuring the bright star Regulus at its heart, lying almost exactly on the ecliptic.

Southern Cross

Crux — the smallest constellation by area, used for navigation in the Southern Hemisphere for centuries by sailors.
